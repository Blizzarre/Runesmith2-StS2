## Runesmith 2 2026-08-03 Changelog (v0.10.1)

### Content and Balances
- Curtain of Flames adjusted/buffed: Gain 6(8) Brace. Gain 3(4) Ignis. => Gain 6(8) Block. Gain 3(4) Brace. Gain 2(3) Ignis.
- White Balance buffed: If you have at least 1 of each Element, gain 1 Energy. ⇒ If you have at least 1 of each Element, gain 1 Energy. Otherwise, gain 1 of each Element.
- Hammer Swing buffed: Deal 5(6) damage ⇒ Deal 6(7) damage.
- Chroma Chisel reworked: Deal 6 damage. Gain 1(2) of each Element. Put a random Recipe card from your Draw Pile into your Hand.
- Flowstate buffed: Gain 3 Amp ⇒ Gain 4 Amp.
- Metal Forming buffed: Gain Block equals to 2(3) times your Terra. ⇒ 3(4) times your Terra.
- Shining Hammer buffed: Deal 11(15) damage ⇒ Deal 13(18) damage.
- Frozen Chisel buffed: Apply 4(6) Ice-Cold ⇒ Apply 5(7) Ice-Cold.
- Electromagnet buffed: Gain 2(5) Block ⇒ Gain 3(6) Block. Put an Improved card from your Discard Pile into your hand. ⇒ Put a Recipe card or an Improved card from your Discard Pile into your Hand.
- Electrocute buffed: Deal 7(10) damage ⇒ Deal 8(11) damage.
- Aetus buffed: Cost 3 Aqua ⇒ Cost 2 Aqua.
- Dry Ice buffed: Gain 1 Aqua ⇒ Gain 1(2) Aqua.
- Quick Charge buffed: Now also give 1 Charge to ALL Runes with 0 Charges.
- Energy Sink adjusted: Now Cost 1 (Upgrades to 0). Base version no longer reduces Runes Potency.
- Hot Hammer adjusted: Deal 6(9) damage => Deal 8(10) damage. Enhance 2 cards at random ⇒ Enhance 1 card at random (random removed on upgrade.)
- Removed Hit and Run card.
- Added a new card: Digital Hammer - 1 Cost Common Attack - Deal 5(6) damage. Put 1 card from your Discard Pile on top of your Draw Pile and Enhance it (by 2).
- Recycler relic nerfed: No longer gains Elements based on the broken Runes. 

### Misc
- Steaming Chisel and Prisma now gains Block before dealing damage.
- Calm Before Storm renamed to Calm Before the Storm (internal name/id remains the same)
