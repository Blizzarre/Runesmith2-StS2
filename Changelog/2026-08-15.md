## Runesmith 2 2026-08-15 Changelog (v0.11.1)

### Content and Balances
- Added new card art for Strike, Defend, Fortify, and Flamma
- Gladius adjusted: Now also gains 1 Energy. Deal 29(36) damage ⇒ 28(34) damage.
- Overload adjusted: Will now always activate Rune's passive and Break it instead of needing at least 5 Charges on the Rune.
- Elemental Decay adjusted: Now gain 1 of each Element for each card Exhausted instead of gaining Elements equal to the Exhausted card Energy costs.
- Quick Charge nerfed/reverted: No longer give Charge to Runes with 0 Charges.
- Rune Hurl nerfed: Deal 8(11) damage ⇒ 6(9) damage.
- Creator Form nerfed: Enhance card by 3(4) ⇒ 2(3).
- Charged Hammer nerfed: Deal 12(14) damage ⇒ 11(13) damage.
- Removed Energy Sink card.
- Added a new card: Superconductor - 1 Cost Uncommon Attack - Deal 8(11) damage. Break your oldest Rune to gain Energy equal to the Rune's Charge.
- Albus Rune nerfed: No longer give Potency. Breaking the Rune will now only give Charges to non-Albus Runes. 
- Iron Supplement potion nerfed: Enhance a card by 5 ⇒ 3.
- Crystallized Rainbow relic nerfed: Every 4 ⇒ 5 Energy spent, gain 1 of each Element.

### Misc
- Added data collection for Runesmith runs. You can disable data collection in the mod settings. (The collected data is anonymized.)
