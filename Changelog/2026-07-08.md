## Runesmith 2 2026-07-08 Changelog (v0.8.3)

### Content and Balances
- Gemma nerfed: Enhance 1 card by 2(3) ⇒ by 1(2).
- Removed MP card: Watch Out!
- Added a new MP card: Lightning Hammer - 1 Cost Uncommon Attack - This card has Stasis. Deal 10(13) damage. Put this card into a random ally's Hand.
- Added a new MP card: Lend a Hand - 1 Cost Uncommon Skill - Enhance ALL cards in another player's Hand (and Stasis them).
- Added a new MP card: One for Everyone - 1(0) Cost Uncommon Skill - Break your oldest Rune. Give each ally a copy of the Broken Rune and Break them.
- Added a new MP card: Conduit - 2(1) Cost Rare Power - Choose an ally. Whenever you Enhance a card, a random card in their Hand gains Enhance by the same amount.

### Misc
- Heirloom Hammer is now considered to be a Hammer card for Shining Hammer.
- Clarified wording for Scrapper power being only for your Runes.
- Updated beta art for Adamantium Hammer.
