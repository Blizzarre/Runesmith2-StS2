## Runesmith 2 2026-08-05 Changelog (v0.10.2)

### Content and Balances
- Particle Accelerator now also function with Rune passive triggers from cards like Quick Charge, Charged Hammer, and Gamma Ray Burst.
  (Overload doesn't matter since it's triggering until the Rune run out of charge anyways.)
  - Due to this change, the additional triggers of Rune passives now occur as an additional loop through all the Runes instead of each Rune getting triggerred multiple times followed by the next Rune.
  - Albus Runes still trigger before other Runes at the end of turn (but not during manual triggers).
- Gemma (token from Breaking Mundus) adjusted: Cost is reduced by 1 on upgrade instead of getting +1 Enhance amount.
- Refine buffed: Now also give the Enhanced card single turn Retain.

### Misc
- Fixed hover tip for Origo+.
- Restored White Balance card glowing gold when you have 1 of each Element (to activate energy gain effect).
