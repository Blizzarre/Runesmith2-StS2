## Runesmith 2 2026-08-20 Changelog (v0.11.2)

### Content and Balances
- Double Up buffed: Gain 10 Block ⇒ Gain 11(12) Block.
- Shining Hammer buffed: Now also Enhances itself instead of just other Hammer cards.
- Quick Charge buffed: Now also gain 1 Amp if you have no Runes.
- Assembler buffed: Now can also target Recipe cards in Hand.
- Electrocute buffed: Deal 8(11) ⇒ 9(12) damage.
- Saxum buffed: Crafted with 3 ⇒ 4 Charges.
- Adamantium Hammer buffed: Deal 6 ⇒ 8 damage.
- Digital Hammer adjusted: Deal 5(6) ⇒ 6(9) damage. Enhance amount is no longer increased on upgrade.
- Hammer and Chisel card removed.

### Misc
- Enhance will now go away after every card play during Replay instead of happening on the final Replay. Stasis will help preserve the Enhance amount.
- Made the Enhanced hover tip show the '+' number of Enhance.
- Adjusted the wording of keywords related to Runes and tips on the Runes.

### Bug Fixes
- Fixed Brace not getting decremented properly in certain situation with Curtain of Flames.
