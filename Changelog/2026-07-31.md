## Runesmith 2 2026-07-31 Changelog (v0.10.0)

### Content and Balances
This patch aims to address cards that do a lot while having great stats already. Cards giving Amp are nerfed due to Amp effect being boosted by Enhance. A10 win is still quite possible albeit slightly harder.
- Reduce Entropy nerfed: Gain 10(12) Block ⇒ Gain 8(10) Block.
- Calm Before Storm nerfed: Draw 2(3) cards next turn ⇒ Draw 1(2) cards next turn.
- Adamantium hammer nerfed: Now cost 3 Energy (upgrade to cost 2). Base damage is 6 instead of 5 (does not increase on upgrade.)
- Gladius adjusted/nerfed: Now cost 3 3 3 Elements instead of 4 4 4 Elements. Deal 40(50) damage ⇒ Deal 29(36) damage.
- Fulgor nerfed: Crafted with 5(7) Potency ⇒ 4(5) Potency.
- Hammer Tornado nerfed: Deal 10(13) damage ⇒ Deal 9(12) damage. Hits an additional time for each Rune ⇒ Hits an additional time for each unique Rune you have.
- Dry Ice nerfed: Gain 4(5) Block ⇒ Gain 4 Block.
- Makeshift Armor nerfed: Gain 7(10) Block ⇒ Gain 6(8) Block.
- Resupply nerfed: Draw 3(4) cards ⇒ Draw 2(3) cards.
- Grindstone nerfed: Gain 6 Block ⇒ Gain 5 Block.
- Runic Forcefield nerfed: Gain 9(13) Block ⇒ Gain 8(11) Block.
- Fission Hammer nerfed: Deal 11(13) damage ⇒ Deal 10(12) damage.
- Hunker Down nerfed: Gain 12(16) Block ⇒ Gain 12(15) Block.
- Flowstate nerfed/adjusted: Gain 4(6) Amp ⇒ Gain 3 Amp. Draw 1 card ⇒ Draw 1(2) cards.
- Chroma Chisel nerfed: Gain 2(3) Amp ⇒ Gain 1(2) Amp.
- Perfect Chisel nerfed: Runes gain 3(4) Potency ⇒ gain 2(3) Potency.
- Fortify nerfed: Gain 6 Block ⇒ Gain 4 Block.
- Architect's Forge nerfed: Enchant with Forged 2(3) ⇒ 1(2).
- Rune Hurl nerfed: Deal 10(14) damage ⇒ 8(11) damage.
- Origo adjusted: 2 2 2 Elements cost ⇒ 1 1 1 Elements cost.
- Origo Rune nerfed: Generated cards are no longer free.
- Scrap (Token card from Scrapper) nerfed: Base version now return to Hand on the next turn. Upgraded version is returned immediately and no longer Break Rune twice.
- Augmented Body adjusted: No longer Stasis cards in Hand on upgrade. Cost is reduced by 1 on upgrade instead.
